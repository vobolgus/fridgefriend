The root cause

People don't know what's in their fridge, forget expiry dates, struggle to decide what to cook — then go shopping again and buy duplicates. No single app solves all of this together.

Solution

One app. Four core capabilities.

Smart Inventory

Photograph your fridge, scan a barcode, or add manually. AI identifies items and expiry dates automatically.

Expiry Tracking

Intelligent notifications before items go bad, ranked by urgency so nothing gets overlooked.

Recipe Engine

AI-ranked recipes biased toward expiring ingredients. Cook what you have before buying more.

Meal Planning

3–7 day plans with a smart shopping list that shows only what the household actually needs to buy.

Real-time household sync — all family members share one fridge view, updated live across devices via server-sent events.
